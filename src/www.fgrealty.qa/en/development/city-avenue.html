City Avenue Residences is a boutique residential landmark developed by Ariane Real Estate in the prestigious Legtaifiya (Al Qassar / West Bay Lagoon area) of Doha. Featuring elegant 1, 2, 3, and 4-bedroom luxury apartments, Milos combines contemporary architectural design with sophisticated interiors, acoustic soundproofing, floor-to-ceiling double glazing, and expansive private balconies with views toward Katara Cultural Village and the West Bay coastline. Residents enjoy an infinity pool, fully equipped fitness center, steam room, children's play lounge, and 24/7 security concierge.
